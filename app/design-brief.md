# Classic Hotel Ijebu-Ode — independent concept demo

- Design read: A refined, approachable mobile-first hotel landing page for people checking a hotel before contacting it.
- Concept spine: A calm, guided arrival from first impression to direct enquiry.
- Delivery tier: cinema-inspired editorial landing page with responsive CSS motion and clear calls to action.
- Animation mode: non-animated — the scaffold was created without the scroll-scrub template; this implementation uses restrained CSS motion instead of claiming a scroll-controlled film.
- Locked palette: forest green #172D26, warm cream #F3F0E7, sage #B7C4A4, ink #202820. Chosen to feel grounded and hospitable.
- Locked type: DM Sans for utility and Playfair Display for editorial headlines.
- Sections: hero, introduction, illustrative room/pool/dining imagery, listed facilities, location, contact CTA, footer.
- Asset plan: hotel and lifestyle photos are illustrative stock photos and are explicitly not represented as actual Classic Hotel photographs; custom C monogram favicon.
- CTA inventory: enquire via WhatsApp; explore; open directions; call hotel.
- Trust rule: conspicuous notice that this is an independent concept, not the hotel's official or approved website. Only publicly listed address, phone and facilities are presented as facts. Rates, room categories and booking availability must be confirmed directly.
